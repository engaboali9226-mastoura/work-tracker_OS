## Registration States

- DRAFT — incomplete and non-operational.
- QUARANTINED — blocked by missing, conflicting, unsafe, or over-capable data.
- REVIEW_READY — complete enough for independent review but not approved.
- APPROVED — permitted only by a later explicit authorization.
- REJECTED — reviewed and not approved.
- WITHDRAWN — removed by the registrar before or after review.
- DEPRECATED — retained for history but not eligible for new use.
- REVOKED — previously approved but explicitly invalidated.
