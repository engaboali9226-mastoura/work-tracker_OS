## Mandatory Semantic Controls

- Step 028 defines the operational candidate registration contract but creates no operational candidate record.
- Every later candidate must bind to exactly one execution-control domain and one Step 027 concept-family lineage.
- Local installation or host availability cannot create implicit registration or approval.
- Missing, ambiguous, stale, conflicting, unsupported, or undeclared information remains registration-blocking.
- Undeclared capability is prohibited rather than inferred as absent.
- Registration planning remains separate from evidence collection, recommendation, selection, implementation, and execution.
- Development support remains distinct from production approval.
- Every later record requires immutable revisions, supersession links, canonical serialization, and content-addressed seals.
- Every later operational registration requires independent review and explicit authorization.
- Step 028 performs no local or external evidence collection, experiment, probe, installation, or candidate execution.
- Step 028 performs no DNS, network, browser, PDF, screenshot, process, or Adapter execution.
