## Mandatory Semantic Controls

- A sealed collection authorization is required before any external source access.
- Official primary sources are preferred; secondary sources require explicit justification.
- Unrelated hosts, redirects, trackers, advertisements, analytics, and third-party assets are outside scope.
- Raw source bytes must be captured before interpretation when legally and technically permitted.
- Screenshots and PDF pages must remain separate from parsed text and carry precise source locations.
- Marketing language must never be converted into a verified security guarantee.
- Freshness revalidation triggers must cover version, host, license, maintenance, and security changes.
- Critical unresolved conflicts remain gate-blocking and cannot be resolved by convenience.
- Evidence collection alone approves no dependency, mechanism, production host, or Adapter.
- Development validation must remain distinct from production enforcement.
- Scores, rankings, recommendations, and selected mechanisms are forbidden evidence fields.
- Every collection run must finish with a sealed non-mutation and authorization attestation.
